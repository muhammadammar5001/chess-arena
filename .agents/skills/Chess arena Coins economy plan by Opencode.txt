Chess Arena — Coin Economy Plan
Abhi code mein economy weak hai: boards free hain, saari 5 abilities unlocked hain, XP/level system nahi hai, aur AdService mock mode pe init hota hai. Isliye pehle earning + spending + progression ka proper loop banani hogi.

Core Rules
1 soft currency: Coins + XP/Level (level sirf unlock eligibility ke liye, coins se nahi khareeda ja sakta).
Cosmetics only — boards, frames, emotes, piece sets. Koi stat boost ya pay-to-win nahi.
Ads optional aur capped — match ke beech mein kabhi nahi.
Ranked PvP fair rahega — hints/undo ranked mein off, ability charges ranked mein free.
New player: 1,000 coins + 2 abilities unlocked.

Match rules
Sirf 2 abilities equip.
Har ability 1 baar per match.
Charges:
Start 3 charges per ability
Max 5
1 use = 1 charge
Daily free +1 charge to all unlocked abilities
Refill: 50 coins/charge ya 120 coins/3 charges
Rewarded ad: +2 charges
AI / casual matches: charges consume hoti hain.
Ranked PvP: charges consume nahi hoti; har selected ability match mein 1 baar free. Isse ranked fair rahega.
Agar aap all modes mein charges chahte hain: start 5 charges, daily +2, refill 75/180. Lekin main launch ke liye recommended nahi karta — players ability use karna chhor denge.

Revenue sanity check
Agar rewarded eCPM roughly $8–$15 hai, to ek completed 30s ad ka revenue approx $0.008–$0.015 hota hai. 100 coins per ad ka matlab roughly $0.01 ad revenue per 100 coins. Board 9,000 coins sirf ads se nahi, matches/missions se bhi unlock hoga — isliye players ko 90 ads grind nahi karna padega.

Target: 2–4 rewarded ads per active user/day, 6 sirf cap hai.

6. More Economy / Fun Ideas
Board Mastery
Har board se 10 wins = gold trim; 50 wins = animated shimmer. Purane boards ki value badhti hai, bina naya content banaye.
Daily rotating shop
Har din 2–3 cosmetics, ek 20% off. Coin sink + urgency.
Alternate piece sets
Classic, Obsidian, Marble, Royal Gold — 5,000–15,000 coins. Ye biggest endgame sink hoga.
Weekly Crown Cup
500 coin entry, 8-player bracket, prizes 3,000 / 1,500 / 750. Entry fees prize pool se zyada hon, taake coins sink hon.
Comeback bonus
3 losses in a row = 100 coins ya 1 free hint. Churn kam karega.
Rematch bonus
Rematch karne par +20 coins. Social retention.
Daily missions with variety
“Play 3 matches”, “Win 1 match”, “Use 1 ability”, “Capture 10 pieces”. Har din different.
No loot boxes
YouTube policies aur player trust ke liye direct cosmetic purchases better hain.
Endgame Royal Vault
20,000–50,000 coins ka prestige board/piece set. Veterans ke coins absorb karega.
7. Inflation Control
AI rewards ko daily cap karo — warna bot farming se inflation hogi.
Har 4–6 weeks naya cosmetic sink add karo.
Board prices ko daily income ke against tune karo:
Ember ≈ 1 day
Moonlit ≈ 2.5 days
Sakura ≈ 4 days
Walnut ≈ 6 days
Ebony ≈ 9 days
Median coin balance track karo. Agar median 20,000+ ho jaye, naya high-tier sink launch karo.
Ads se direct coins hi do, “ad = free board” nahi — warna coin currency useless ho jayegi.
8. Technical Notes Before Implementation
Profile mein add karna hoga:
level, xp
unlockedAbilities, abilityCharges
unlockedBoards
adDailyCount, lastAdReset
dailyMissions, loginStreak
In sab fields ko sanitize/clamp karna hoga.
Leaderboard sync coins ko overwrite na kare — coins local/cloud profile mein rahen, warna match sync se balance ud sakta hai.
Ad reward idempotent ho: rewardId store karo, taake reconnect par double reward na mile.
AdService ko PlayGama/YouTube adapter auto-detect karna hoga; abhi default mock hai.
Hint/Undo ranked mein disable.
Economy values ek central config file mein rakho, taake baad mein tune karna easy ho.
Recommended Launch Order
Phase 1: Coins from matches, daily missions, login, rewarded ads, new hint/undo costs, board prices, ability unlock/refill.

Phase 2: XP/levels, daily shop, board mastery, win streaks.

Phase 3: Piece sets, tournaments, seasonal vault.

Final Recommendation
Launch with the balanced version:

100 coins/ad, max 6/day
Hints 25/50/100/200
Boards 1,200–9,000 + level gates
2 starting abilities
3 charges, 50 coins/charge, ranked free
No pay-to-win in ranked
Cosmetics + boards as main sinks
Agar yeh direction approve hai, main Phase 1 implement kar sakta hoon: profile fields, ad rewards, board prices, ability unlock/refill UI, daily caps, aur ranked restrictions.
