YouTube Playables aur Playgama ke model mein Rewarded Video Ads sabse zyada revenue generate karte hain (Tier-1 countries mein $15–$35+ eCPM milta hai).

Ads revenue ko maximize karne ka golden rule yeh hai: Player ko zabardasti ads na dikhayein, balki player khud faida dekh kar khushi khushi ad button dabaye.

Aapke poore economy system ka detailed balanced plan yeh raha:

1. Coin Economy: Faucets (Earning) & Sinks (Spending)
Action / Source	Coins	Type / Condition
Welcome Bonus	+300 🪙	New player first launch (enough to test features)
Match Win (PvP/AI)	+40 🪙	Organic gameplay reward
Match Loss	+10 🪙	Consolation reward (demotivate na ho)
🎬 Rewarded Video Ad	+150 🪙	Direct "Watch Ad for Coins" button in Shop/Home
🎬 3x Match Win Bonus	+120 🪙	Match jeetne ke baad: "Watch Ad to 3X your Win (+120 🪙)"
Daily Tavern Login	+50 🪙	Retention booster (24h cooldown)
2. Hints & Doubledown Cost System
Chess mein "Best Move Hint" bohot powerful cheez hoti hai, isliye iska cost match ke andar progressive (Double-Down) hona chahiye:

1st Hint in match: 25 🪙 (Impulse buy, har koi use karega)
2nd Hint in match: 50 🪙 (Double-down)
3rd Hint in match: 100 🪙 (Double-down)
4th+ Hint in match: 200 🪙 (Capped, taake poora balance ek match mein wipe out na ho)
Alternative Direct Ad: Agar player ke paas coins na hon: "Watch 1 quick Ad for an Instant Grandmaster Hint".
3. Boards Unlock Economy (Progression + Ad Revenue)
Boards ko sirf coins par nahi, balki Levels aur Direct Ad Watches ke combination par unlock karna best hai:

Board Name	Unlock Requirement	Monetization Strategy
Classic Oak	Free (Default)	Starter board
Walnut & Maple	350 🪙 (or Level 3)	Early goal (1-2 matches + 1 Ad)
Emberwood	600 🪙 (or Level 5)	Mid-tier goal (4 Ads ya gameplay)
Moonlit Grove	900 🪙 (or Level 8)	High-tier prestige
Sakurawood	1,500 🪙 (or Level 12)	Long-term coin sink
Royal Ebony & Ivory	🎬 3 Ads Milestone	"Watch 3 Ads total to unlock permanently" (100% Ad Conversion)
4. Skills Economy: Levels, Loadout & Refills
A. Starting vs Locked Skills:
Start mein 2 Skills Unlocked rahengi:
Pegasus Jump: (Game ka core fun factor hai, shuru se hona chahiye).
Undo / Second Chance: (Beginners ke blunders bachane ke liye zaroori hai).
Baqi 3 Skills Level-Locked hongi:
Freeze Skill: Unlock at Level 4 (+ 200 🪙 unlock fee).
Swap Skill: Unlock at Level 7 (+ 400 🪙 unlock fee).
Divine Shield: Unlock at Level 10 (+ 600 🪙 unlock fee).
B. Loadout & Match Rules (Aapke mutabiq):
Match shuru karne se pehle player apne loadout mein sirf 2 Skills select kar sakega.
Ek match mein har equipped skill sirf 1 baar use ho sakegi.
C. Skill Refills (Constant Coin Sink):
Har skill ke paas Charges (Bottles) hongi (Max capacity: 5 charges). Match mein use karne par 1 charge consume hoga.

Single Refill (1 Charge): 40 🪙
Full Pack Refill (5 Charges): 150 🪙 (Save 50 coins — exactly 1 Ad value!)
🎬 Free 0-Charge Recovery: Agar kisi skill ke 0 charges ho jayein:
Button par aayega: 🎬 Watch Ad for +3 Charges Free. (Ye button sabse zyada clicks lega!)
5. Game ko mazeed Fun aur Profitable banane ke 4 Unique Ideas:
"Lucky Tavern Wheel" (Daily Spinner):
Har roz 1 Free spin.
Uske baad har extra spin par 1 Rewarded Ad.
Prizes: 50 🪙, 100 🪙, 250 🪙 Jackpot, +2 Skill Charges, 1-Hour Board Trial.
Win-Streak Protector (Loss Aversion):
Jab player 3+ matches lagataar jeet chuka ho aur agla match haarne lage:
Popup: "Protect your 3-Win Streak! Watch an Ad to keep the streak alive." (Psychology: Log apni streak bachane ke liye foran ad dekhte hain).
Revenge Match Boost:
AI ya online match haarne par rematch screen par:
"Watch Ad to Rematch with +1 Free Tactical Skill Charge".
Tavern Trophy Titles (Bragging Rights):
Profile par Titles jo rating aur level se unlock hon (e.g. Apprentice, Tavern Knight, Grandmaster). No coins needed, pure motivation to keep playing.
Implementation Overview (Jab aap decide karein ge):
State Manager (StorageService): Player data schema mein coins, level, skillCharges: { jump: 3, undo: 3, ... } add karenge.
Shop & Loadout UI: Shop screen par Refill Packs aur Boards ke Ad counters add karenge, aur match start se pehle 2-Skill Loadout selector banayenge.
Playgama Ad Hooks: AdService.showRewarded() call karke coins aur refills instantly credit karenge.
Yeh plan aapko kaisa laga? Isme koi number ya price kam/zyada karni ho to batayein, aur jab aap bolein ge ke "ye implement krdo", hum is par kaam start kar denge!
