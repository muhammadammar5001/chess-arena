# Wooden Chess: Future Suggestions & Enhancements

These recommendations are documented for future releases and did not alter core gameplay during the redesign.

1. **Acoustic Wooden Sound Pack:**
   - Add high-quality audio samples of solid wood pieces clicking on an oak board for moves, captures, check, and castling.
2. **Streamer Emote Wheel:**
   - Expand the in-game emote buttons with animated wood-carved owl reactions ("GG", "Thinking...", "Well Played!").
3. **Daily Login & Streak Chests:**
   - Award daily login gold coins to incentivize return play on YouTube Playables.
4. **Theme Customizer in Settings:**
   - Allow players to toggle coordinates on/off and switch between Oak, Mahogany, and Burl themes directly from the in-game settings modal.
