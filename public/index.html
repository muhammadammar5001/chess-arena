How to Play

🎯 Objective

Checkmate your opponent's king — trap it so it cannot escape capture.

♟️ Pieces

♔King — 1 square any direction
♕Queen — Any direction, any distance
♖Rook — Horizontal & vertical
♗Bishop — Diagonals only
♘Knight — L-shape, can jump
♙Pawn — Forward 1 (2 from start), captures diagonally

✨ Special Moves

Castling: King moves 2 squares toward a rook; the rook jumps over.

En Passant: Capture a pawn that just double-moved past your pawn.

Promotion: A pawn reaching the far rank becomes a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

🎮 Controls

Tap a piece to select it, then tap a highlighted square to move.

⏱️ Match Timers & Fair Play

10-Minute Clock: Total match time per player.

45-Second Move Timer: You have 45 seconds to make each move. While your opponent considers their move, "Opponent is thinking..." with their countdown is displayed.

Inactivity (AFK) Rules: Missing a move within 45s passes your turn to the opponent with an AFK Warning (1st Strike). Missing a 2nd time results in an automatic forfeit loss.
